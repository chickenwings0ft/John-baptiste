ROUND 00 / WARM-UP

Diagnosis & Foundations

Before anything gets published, we define positioning and audit what already exists.

→

Define the niche

  • Weight loss, strength, women 40+, athletic performance, body recomposition, physique coaching, discipline-driven online coaching? Pick one or two lanes to start — we can broaden from there.
→

Define the ideal client

  • Age, lifestyle, the exact problem they want solved, and what's stopping them today.
→

Audit existing assets

  • Current Instagram/Facebook, Google reviews, testimonials, before/after photos already sitting on a phone.

All of this will be filled in through the final questionnaire.

ROUND 01 / BUILD

A Presence Built to Convert

Every channel has one job: move someone closer to booking a session.

01

Landing page

  • Value proposition, testimonials, before/after, one clear CTA — trial session, form, or WhatsApp.
  • Google Analytics and Meta Pixel installed day one. Analyze the data to perform better.
02

Google Business Profile

  • Category: Coaching. Fixed location (your home), service-area, or online services, depending on where sessions happen.
  • Photos, services, and reviews stacking from client one.
03

Instagram

  • Three content pillars: Authority (tips, technique), Proof (transformations, testimonials), Personality (behind the scenes).
  • Reels first — that's where organic reach lives right now.
04

Facebook

  • Mirrors Instagram content, doubles as the ad platform. Local groups for extra organic reach.
  • TikTok / YouTube Shorts and Reddit for better performance. Same content distributed across every platform at the same time.
ROUND 02 / PAID PUSH

Facebook & Instagram Ads

First moves — while we build a relevant organic positioning, we'll use ads as a fast lane for lead acquisition (3–6 months). After that, we can analyze the organic positioning built on the website and social media, and adjust the ads accordingly.

→

The offer

  • Free trial session, free fitness assessment, or a 7-day challenge — not a hard sell.
→

Campaign setup

  • Lead Generation or Traffic-to-landing-page with a form.
  • Start local: geographic radius + fitness/coaching/weight-loss interests, refine with real data after. Then personalized ads and landing pages adapted to user queries and ad performance.
→

Creative & budget

  • John's videos, edited with clear calls to action, strong branding and personality.
  • Test across 2–3 variations before scaling spend.
